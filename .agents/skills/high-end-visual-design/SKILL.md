# Intentional visual quality

Start from the audience, primary task, content, and brand. For existing UI, use the redesign audit; for new UI, establish a direction from the brief. Product tools, commerce, and marketing need different densities and information structures.

- **Hierarchy:** Distinguish title, key information, primary action, and supporting content through size, weight, proximity, and contrast. Keep necessary information; do not turn complex product UI into marketing snippets.
- **Spacing:** Reuse or establish a small consistent scale. Align baselines/gutters and related groups. Fix cramped/disconnected elements before enlarging sections. Avoid doubling whitespace everywhere.
- **Typography:** Use brand-compatible font roles, responsive scale, readable measure, and deliberate line-height. Protect diacritics/descenders and long translations. Use tabular figures for aligned numeric data where appropriate. Do not assume commercial fonts are available.
- **Layout:** Use predictable grids for comparable items and logical DOM/read order. Asymmetry should serve content and survive mobile. Do not randomize layouts or force bento, masonry, centered heroes, or split screens everywhere.
- **Imagery:** Reserve dimensions, choose intentional ratios/crops, and handle missing media cleanly. Preserve truthful product/content images. Use existing real assets before adding unrelated decoration.
- **Surfaces:** Keep coherent radius, border, shadow, and layering roles. Align nested curves. Containers should group information; glass, grain, pills, and double-bezel are optional tools rather than quality requirements.
- **Actions:** Make the primary action clear without hiding secondary paths. Preserve readable labels, accessible states, and responsive feedback. Avoid decorative effects that compete with work or delay interaction.

Compare rendered reading priority, alignment, visual competition, and mobile usability with the brief/baseline. Remove competing decoration; a checklist or mockup alone does not prove implementation quality.
